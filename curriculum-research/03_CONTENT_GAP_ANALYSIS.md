## 1. The core distinction: standards vs. teachable content

Two different things are often conflated:

- **The standards** ("*what* to teach") — subjects, domains, and Student Learning Outcomes.
- **The teachable content** ("*how* we teach it") — the lessons, explanations, worked examples, images,
  audio, and quiz items a child actually uses.

**The public availability of these two is opposite:**

| Layer | Publicly available? | Legally reusable as-is? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standards (SLOs, Scheme of Studies, assessment frameworks)** | ✅ **~100%** (free PDFs on ncc.gov.pk / fbise) | ⚠️ reference yes; verbatim ingest needs MoU | **Available to align to** |
| **Teachable content (lessons, items, media)** | free-to-view textbooks/videos exist | ❌ **~0% legally reusable** (all ARR/PROP; open bits are NC-restricted) | **Must be authored** |

> **Bottom line:** the *hardest strategic input* (a validated national standards spine) is essentially
> **free and done**; the *largest labor input* (original teaching content + item bank) is essentially
> **all ours to build**. This is the correct, defensible shape for a world-class school and requires
> **no publisher partnership**.

## 3. The authoring workload (planning estimate)

Labeled planning assumptions; confirm against the actual Progression Grids.

| Artifact | Scale (KG–10) | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Grade-subject cells | ~80–90 | [02 §3](./02_MASTER_CURRICULUM_MATRIX.md) |
| SLOs | ~5,000–12,000 | ~6–12 units/cell × 3–8 SLOs/unit |
| Lesson objects | ~5,000–12,000+ | ≥1 per SLO (often several blocks) |
| **Assessment items** | ~**30,000–90,000** | ≥5× distinct-item pool per SLO (mastery + anti-gaming, [58 §1/§4](../docs/05-education/58-mastery-and-assessment-validity.md)) |
| Urdu audio assets | thousands | mandatory recorded audio for core-path text ([16](../docs/04-design/16-accessibility-standards.md)) |

**This item-bank number is the single largest hidden content cost** (flagged in
[WORLD_CLASS_GAP_ANALYSIS](../WORLD_CLASS_GAP_ANALYSIS.md)) and must be modeled before committing grade
bands.

## 4. Two accelerators (reduce the build, legally)

1. **NCC / MoFEPT MoU** — the highest-leverage move. Written permission to ingest the official Scheme of
   Studies + Progression Grids converts layer 2 from "re-express by hand" to "ingest + structure"
   (pipeline [04](./04_CURRICULUM_INGESTION_PIPELINE.md)), saving significant standards-authoring and
   guaranteeing recognition alignment. **Likely grantable** for a non-profit child-education mission.
2. **AI-assisted authoring (human-reviewed)** — use AI to *draft* lesson/quiz objects *to our own
   SLO-aligned taxonomy*, with mandatory human psychometric + safety + bias review before publish
   ([21 §6](../docs/05-education/21-curriculum-engine.md), [58 §4](../docs/05-education/58-mastery-and-assessment-validity.md)).
   This is drafting *original* content aligned to public standards — **not** copying textbooks. It can
   cut item-bank authoring time by a large factor while keeping quality gated by humans.

**Genuinely-open supplements** (Khan Academy Math/Science, ⚠️ NonCommercial caveat → counsel + per-asset
verification) can *supplement* Grades 6–10 Math/Science reference, but cannot be the spine and cannot be
assumed usable in a commercial context.

## 5. Recommended sourcing strategy (per layer)

| Layer | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Standards / SLOs | **Align to public NCP** (free); pursue **NCC/MoFEPT MoU** to ingest verbatim; else re-express (counsel-reviewed) |
| Lessons | **Author original**, AI-drafted + human-reviewed, SLO-aligned, Urdu-first with mandatory audio |
| Items | **Author original** item bank (≥5×/SLO), AI-assisted + psychometric review; FBISE model papers as *reference only* |
| Media | Original or CC0/openly-licensed only; never copyrighted textbook images |
| Teacher pedagogy | Reference public teacher guides (KP SNC LPs, NCF) to inform our own |
| Context data | Reuse open datasets (ASER/PSLM/PIE/WB) per license for the OOS flag + targeting + impact |
| Textbooks (PCTB/STBB/NBF) | **Reference-for-alignment only**; ingest verbatim **only** under written permission; **never** third-party scans |

## 6. Answer to "how much already exists"

- **Standards spine: ~100% exists publicly and is free** to align to (the strategically hardest input).
- **Legally-reusable teaching content: ~0%** — the entire lesson + item corpus must be authored (with the
  two accelerators above).
- **No commercial publisher partnership is required.** The critical *optional* partnership is a
  **standards-access MoU with NCC/MoFEPT**, which accelerates and de-risks (recognition) but does not gate
  building a curriculum-aligned school.
