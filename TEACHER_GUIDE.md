# Teacher & Mentor Guide — Grade 4 Mathematics Pilot

Status: **Content deliverable (WS4/WS7-facing). Plan only.** Guidance for the mentors/teachers who
facilitate the Grade 4 Mathematics pilot. It accompanies the
[curriculum](GRADE4_MATH_CURRICULUM.md), the [lesson catalog](LESSON_CATALOG.md), and the
[audio guide](AUDIO_SCRIPT_GUIDE.md). It is **content + facilitation guidance**, not a product feature,
and does not change any platform code.

Mentors are **central**, not optional: the AI is a patient helper, but **you** teach, judge mastery at
summative, and are the human the child reaches when they need one.

---

## 1. Your role in the pilot

- **Facilitate, don't lecture.** The app leads each lesson; you circulate, watch, and step in where the
  child needs a human.
- **You own the summative.** The end-of-pilot summative is **mentor-mediated** — you review constructed
  answers and confirm mastery. The computer never promotes a child on its own.
- **You are the safety layer.** Any distress signal or repeated stuck-state escalates to you; your
  response is the child's safeguard. Follow the safeguarding runbook (WS2) at all times.
- **You are the feedback channel.** Your notes on what confused children, what worked, and what to fix
  are a primary pilot output.

---

## 2. How the learning model works (so you can support it)

- **Mastery is evidence-based.** A child "masters" an objective by answering **≥ 4 of the last 5**
  distinct items correctly (pool of ≥ 5). It is not a single test; it is accumulated evidence.
- **Spacing brings topics back.** Mastered objectives re-surface later (spaced re-check) to keep them
  fresh — this is deliberate, not a bug.
- **The AI gives graduated hints, never the answer** — H1 orient, H2 strategy, H3 a worked re-teach.
  After that, it escalates to **you**.
- **Misconceptions are detected and named.** Wrong answers often map to a known misconception (see §4);
  the app responds with a correction. Your job is to reinforce it in person.
- **No high-stakes grading.** Learning signals are formative and private; nothing ranks the child.

---

## 3. Facilitating a session (practical flow)

1. **Before:** confirm devices/audio work; confirm the lesson is downloaded (offline-ready); check
   headphones/volume for the audio-first lessons.
2. **Start:** let each child begin their own lesson; the app reads the Urdu aloud.
3. **Circulate:** watch for children who are stuck *after* hints, frustrated, or racing without
   understanding. Intervene warmly.
4. **When a child is stuck:** ask what they think first; use the lesson's worked example; reinforce the
   named misconception correction — **don't just give the answer** (model the app's approach).
5. **Escalations:** respond promptly to any escalation the app routes to you; a wellbeing signal always
   takes priority over the maths.
6. **After:** note what confused children, any content errors, and any child who needs follow-up.

Keep sessions **short and positive**. Praise effort and persistence, not speed or cleverness.

## 4. Grade 4 misconception quick-reference (what to watch for)

These are the misconceptions the content is built to detect. When you see one in person, reinforce the
correction plainly (full library + child-facing corrections in
[GRADE4_MATH_CURRICULUM.md](GRADE4_MATH_CURRICULUM.md) §4).

| Topic | Watch for | Reinforce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place value | reading a digit's face value, ignoring its column | "the place tells the value" |
| Comparing | "more digits = bigger" without checking | count digits, then left-to-right |
| Addition | forgetting to carry | carry when a column passes 9 |
| Subtraction | flipping digits instead of borrowing | borrow from the next column |
| Multiplication | adding the factors instead of multiplying | repeated equal groups |
| Multiplication (2×2) | missing the placeholder zero | second row starts with a zero |
| Division | thinking the answer gets bigger | dividing shares — result is smaller |
| Division | dropping the remainder | "what is left over?" |
| Fractions | bigger denominator = bigger fraction | more parts = smaller parts |
| Fractions | adding denominators | add tops, keep the bottom |
| Fractions | changing only the numerator for equivalents | multiply top **and** bottom |
| Measurement | mixing units (cm + m) | convert to one unit first |
| Time | swapping hour and minute hands | short = hours, long = minutes |
| Angles | "bigger drawing = bigger angle" | the angle is the opening, not arm length |
| Symmetry | any line counts as symmetry | fold-test: halves must match |
| Graphs | reading bar height, ignoring the scale | read the scale/key first |

## 5. Judging mastery + the summative

- **During the pilot:** the app tracks objective mastery automatically for auto-graded items. Use its
  progress view to see who is ahead and who needs support.
- **Constructed answers** ("explain in your own words", "show your working") are **not auto-graded** —
  review them yourself; look for understanding, not perfect wording.
- **The summative (`S-math-g4-pilot`) is yours to mediate.** Confirm each child's mastery across
  objectives using both the auto-graded evidence and your own judgement of their constructed answers.
  **Never let the system promote a child without your review.**
- **A child who has not mastered an objective is not "failing"** — they simply need more time and
  revision. Route them back through the revision lesson.

---

## 6. Safeguarding (non-negotiable)

- **Every wellbeing or distress signal reaches you — respond within the SLA.** This overrides the
  lesson, always.
- **Follow the safeguarding runbook and mandatory-reporting policy** (WS2). If in doubt, escalate to
  the safeguarding lead — never handle a serious concern alone.
- **Protect privacy:** do not ask children to share personal information; keep records per policy.
- **Be present.** In Pilot 1, mentors and a safeguarding lead are physically present during all
  sessions. Your attention is the primary safety mechanism.

---

## 7. Giving feedback that improves the pilot

Please log, per session:

- **Content issues** — any lesson, item, worked example, or audio segment that was wrong, confusing,
  culturally off, or mis-narrated (feeds [CONTENT_QA_CHECKLIST.md](CONTENT_QA_CHECKLIST.md)).
- **Sticking points** — objectives where many children struggled (may need re-teaching or an extra
  worked example).
- **Engagement** — what held attention, what lost it.
- **Accessibility** — any child who could not access a lesson (audio, reading, device, or disability
  barrier).
- **Safety** — any escalation, how it was handled, and the outcome (per safeguarding reporting).

Your notes are a **primary Pilot-1 deliverable** — they decide what is fixed before Pilot 2.
