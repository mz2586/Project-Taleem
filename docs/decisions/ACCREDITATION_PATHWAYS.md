**Status:** Research complete. Owner decisions are open. · **Prepared:** 2026-10-24 · **Owners:** Founder + Legal +
Education lead · **Scope reference:** [`GLOBAL_PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`](../GLOBAL_PRODUCT_SCOPE.md) § Definitive mission,

## The question

Taleem is an AI-led, online-only school with no hired teaching workforce. This brief answers two questions. Which
credible routes let a child who learns with Taleem obtain a **recognized** qualification? Which of those routes are
compatible with AI-led instruction?

The launch market is Pakistan (KG to Grade 10, National Curriculum of Pakistan). International routes follow.

## Method and evidence rules

- Primary sources (exam boards, ministries, regulators, awarding bodies) were preferred. All sources were accessed on
  **2026-10-24**.
- **UNVERIFIED** marks any claim that could not be confirmed from a primary source. These claims come from secondary
  sources or from a search snippet of a page that refused automated access, or were not found at all. Treat each one
  as a question to confirm with the body named. Do not repeat it as fact.
- No requirement or fee has been invented. Where a fee is not in a primary source, the brief says so.
- "Compatible with AI-led?" judges whether the route can work **without Taleem employing or depending on teachers**.
  It is not a judgement on whether a route is desirable.

## Summary

1. **The workable route is the private-candidate route.** The certifier examines the child, not the school. Two
   options are FBISE SSC as a private candidate (Grades 9 to 10) and Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel International GCSE
   as a private candidate. Neither primary source we read sets any requirement on how the candidate was taught.
2. **Registering Taleem itself as a school or as an exam-board-approved online school is incompatible with the
   no-teacher model.** In Islamabad, PEIRA registration needs classrooms, a 1:30 teacher-student ratio and B.Ed or
   M.Ed staff. Cambridge's online-school registration needs qualified teachers and lesson observation. England's
   online accreditation scheme needs live teacher supervision.
3. **The biggest gap is KG to Grade 8.** We found no recognized private-candidate certificate in Pakistan for primary
   or middle level that a child outside a registered school can sit (**UNVERIFIED** that none exists). Cambridge
   Primary Checkpoint is closed to private candidates. Below Grade 9, Taleem can offer progress evidence, but it
   cannot offer a recognized credential without a partner.
4. **The residual human or institutional touchpoints are small and can be met by partners.** They are in-person
   invigilation, attestation of the admission form, practical-work certificates, and oral components. None of them is
   instruction.

## The five levels to distinguish

Every public statement, screen and certificate must sit at exactly one of these levels. Claims must never move up a
level without the evidence named in the right-hand column.

| Level | What it means | Who grants it | Can Taleem claim it today? | Evidence needed to claim it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Learning platform | Software that teaches. No status as a school. | Nobody. No permission is needed in most places. | **Yes** | None |
| 2. Structured online school (self-described) | A complete programme with curriculum mapping, progression, records and safeguarding, run by Taleem. It is not authorized by any government. | Self-declared | **Conditional.** Whether an unregistered online provider may call itself a "school" in Pakistan is **UNVERIFIED**. See OD-1. | A legal opinion on use of the word "school" in each launch jurisdiction |
| 3. Authorized institution | Registered or licensed to operate by a government regulator (for example ICT-PEIRA), or affiliated to an exam board, or approved by an awarding body (for example a registered Cambridge school) | Regulator, board or awarding body | **No** | A registration certificate or affiliation letter on file |
| 4. Accredited institution | Quality-assured by an external accreditor (for example Cognia, or England's OEAS) | Accreditor | **No** | An accreditation decision on file |
| 5. Recognized qualification | A certificate issued by a recognized awarding body (FBISE, a BISE, AIOU, Cambridge, Pearson, NIOS) and accepted for progression (for example IBCC equivalence) | Awarding body, plus the equivalence body | **No.** Taleem never awards one. A learner may earn one as a private candidate. | The certificate is issued to the learner by the awarding body |

A learner can hold a Level 5 qualification even when Taleem is only at Level 1 or 2. This is the basis of the
recommended path. Certification is decoupled from instruction.

## Role of the National Curriculum Council

The NCC (Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training) sets curriculum and clears textbooks. It does not
examine or certify learners. On 4 November 2024, ICT-PEIRA instructed all ICT private institutions to ensure "that no
book is being taught in any class without prior clearance through a No Objection Certificate (NOC) issued by the
National Curriculum Council" (S9).

What this means for Taleem:

- Whether Taleem's AI lessons and curriculum packs count as a "book" needing an NCC NOC is **UNVERIFIED**. This is an
  open question for legal counsel (OD-4).
- Alignment with NCP student learning outcomes is what makes FBISE SSC preparation credible. The NCC itself confers
  no recognition on a provider.
- Secondary sources say provincial textbook boards issue NOCs to private publishers, and that federal NCC decisions
  apply to federally administered schools because education is a provincial subject (**UNVERIFIED**).

## Recommended staged path

### Stage 0: what Taleem can truthfully offer now (no authorization needed)

- A free AI-led learning programme mapped to the National Curriculum of Pakistan, KG to Grade 10 (Level 1). Use
  Level 2 wording only after OD-1 is answered.
- Evidence-based progress and mastery records, labelled: "Taleem learning record. This is not a school certificate
  or a qualification."
- Exam-readiness for **FBISE SSC as a private candidate** (Grades 9 to 10). Include guidance on the admission form,
  the affidavit, the B-Form and the fee waiver, and on choosing subjects that avoid unpartnered practicals.
- Exam-readiness for Cambridge and Pearson International GCSE in subjects open to private candidates, for families
  who can pay or are sponsored.
- Allowed wording: "Learners can sit the FBISE SSC as private candidates." Not allowed: "Taleem students receive the
  SSC through Taleem."

### Stage 1: needs a partner, but not authorization of Taleem

- **Practical-work partner** (FBISE-affiliated schools) for science and computer practical certificates (I-3).
- **Attestation partner** (an affiliated institution head) for the private admission form (I-4).
- **Exam-centre partners** (British Council or Cambridge or Pearson centres in Pakistan) for international entries
  (I-6).
- **Non-formal education or ALP partner** for a primary-equivalence certificate (I-12).
- Formalize each partner using the P-1 to P-8 template in
  [`TALEEM_SCHOOL_PARTNER_REQUIREMENTS.md`](../TALEEM_SCHOOL_PARTNER_REQUIREMENTS.md). Note one conflict: P-3 there
  asks for "qualified teachers", which conflicts with the 2026-10-24 mission. That document needs revising
  separately (OD-8).

### Stage 2: needs authorization or regulatory dialogue

- Ask MoFEPT, ICT-PEIRA and the NCC whether an online, AI-led provider can be registered or recognized as a
  non-formal or online institution, and on what standards (OD-2, OD-4).
- Commission an independent evaluation of learning outcomes. A regulator or accreditor will ask for this evidence
  first. The Pennsylvania denial cited an "untested" AI model (S25).
- Revisit Cognia only after reading its current standards and assurances on educator requirements (R14).

### Never claim

- That Taleem is a registered, licensed, affiliated, accredited or recognized school, anywhere, before the
  certificate is on file.
- That Taleem awards, issues or confers SSC, IGCSE or any other qualification, or any "equivalent" of one.
- That a Taleem record is equivalent to a grade pass, a Middle certificate or a school-leaving certificate.
- That Taleem is a Cambridge or Pearson school or centre, or that it is "Cambridge-approved".
- That Taleem certifies practical work, Nazira Quran completion, character, or attendance for any board form.
- That AI-led instruction is approved by any regulator. R16 is one US state's charter decision, not a general
  approval.
