## Purpose

This closes the single highest-liability gap: the blueprint detected distress/abuse but had no defined,
safe way to act on it. It defines tiered escalation with **numeric SLAs**, 24/7 coverage, deterministic
crisis holding responses, and the mandatory-reporting/external-referral pathway — the "safeguarding
escalation policy" that [15 §5](./15-child-safety-framework.md) referenced but did not contain.

## Scope

In scope: signal tiers, response SLAs, staffing/coverage, deterministic holding responses, guardian
handling when the guardian is implicated, and the external-referral decision process. Out of scope: the
detection classifiers ([24](../05-education/24-ai-teacher-specification.md)) and general incident response
([53](../07-engineering/53-incident-response-plan.md)). **SLA numbers and referral channels below are
planning assumptions pending clinical and legal sign-off** — they exist so the gate is testable, not to
assert an approved policy.

---

## 1. Principle

A detected child in crisis is the most important event the platform handles. Detection without a defined,
staffed, safe response is negligence. This protocol makes the response deterministic, timed, and human.

## 2. Signal tiers & response SLAs (planning assumptions — DECISION REQUIRED)

| Tier | Example signal | Target response | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T0 — Imminent harm** | Active self-harm/suicidal intent; disclosure of ongoing abuse | Live trained human **≤ 5 min**, 24/7 | 24/7 always-on |
| **T1 — Acute** | Distress, historical-abuse disclosure, grooming indicators | Human **≤ 30 min**, 24/7 | 24/7 |
| **T2 — Concern** | Bullying, welfare concern, repeated distress language | Human **≤ 4 h** (waking hours) | Extended hours |
| **T3 — Advisory** | Low-confidence flags for review | **≤ 24 h** | Business hours |

These SLAs drive the SLO in [38 Monitoring](../07-engineering/38-monitoring.md) and the staffing model.
**The numbers require sign-off by a child-safeguarding clinician.**

## 3. Deterministic crisis holding response

While a human is engaged, the child sees a **pre-authored, clinician-reviewed, age- and
language-localized template** — **never** free-generated by the LLM ([24](../05-education/24-ai-teacher-specification.md)
updated). Templates:

- validate the child, state a human is coming, and surface a safe local crisis resource;
- are served deterministically outside the model path (so a degraded/jailbroken model cannot alter them);
- are red-teamed and signed off specifically.

## 4. When the guardian is (or may be) the threat

- The guardian is **not** notified of a T0/T1 safeguarding case where the guardian is implicated or
  cannot be excluded (household-adversary model, [51](./51-threat-model.md)).
- The relevant transcript turns are confidential to the guardian/mentor and reachable only via the C4
  Safety-Officer path ([12](./12-authorization-model.md)).
- Guardian data-export is safeguarding-checked ([14](./14-privacy-model.md)).

## 5. Mandatory reporting & external referral (scaffold — DECISION REQUIRED, legal)

The concrete Pakistani reporting channels, mandatory-reporting obligations, and authorization chain
**must be determined by legal counsel + safeguarding leadership before build.** This section scaffolds
the decision structure:

| Element | To be decided |
|---|---|
| Reportable categories | Which disclosures trigger mandatory external referral |
| Channels | Named child-protection agencies / law-enforcement contacts per province |
| Authorizer | Who authorizes an external referral (dual-control) |
| Do-no-harm test | How referral avoids endangering the child (e.g. intra-family abuse, a girl's schooling status) |
| Child protection from retaliation | How the child is shielded during/after referral |
| Recording | Immutable audit of every referral decision ([13 §9](./13-security-model.md)) |

Until this is signed off, **no automated external referral occurs** and cases hold in the C4 zone for
human decision — but detection, holding response, and internal human escalation are fully operational.

## 6. Coverage & staffing dependency

24/7 T0/T1 coverage requires a follow-the-sun or Pakistan-night safeguarding roster. Headcount is
derived in the staffing/capacity model ([53 §4](../07-engineering/53-incident-response-plan.md) references it);
**cohort growth is gated on safeguarding-responder capacity** — the platform does not enrol children it
cannot protect 24/7.
