## 3. Exemplar lesson — ★ ENG-G4-GR-03 (Simple tenses)

- **Learning outcome `[RE-EXPRESSED]`:** the learner can use simple present, past, and future correctly
  in a sentence.
- **Difficulty:** CORE · **Duration:** 18 min · **Prerequisites:** ENG-G4-GR-02.
- **Vocabulary (Urdu support):** present = حال, past = ماضی, future = مستقبل.
- **Worked example (Urdu-first script + English target):** "آج میں کھیلتا ہوں — *I play today* (حال)۔
  کل میں نے کھیلا — *I played yesterday* (ماضی)۔ کل میں کھیلوں گا — *I will play tomorrow* (مستقبل)۔"
- **Practice (≥ 5 items):** choose the correct verb form for a given time word (today/yesterday/
  tomorrow); one "fix the tense" selector. Auto-graded.
- **Assessment:** 6-item pool. **Revision triggers:** `m-tense-time-mismatch`.
- **Misconception:** `m-tense-time-mismatch` — verb form doesn't match the time word → correction: "وقت
  کا لفظ دیکھیں: yesterday → past, tomorrow → future."
- **Hint ladder:** H1 "کونسا وقت؟ (کب)"; H2 "اُس وقت کی فعل کی شکل"; H3 re-teach with play/played/will
  play.
- **Homework:** say one sentence about today, yesterday, and tomorrow to a family member.
- **Parent note:** you don't need English — just ask your child to say "today / yesterday / tomorrow"
  sentences.
- **Teacher note:** anchor tense to a time word; reinforce the correction in person.

## 4. Misconception library (English)

| Ref | Misconception | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `m-tense-time-mismatch` | Verb form doesn't match the time word | match the form to the time word |
| `m-article-a-an` | Uses "a" before a vowel sound | use "an" before a vowel sound |
| `m-plural-add-s-always` | Adds -s to every plural | irregular plurals (child→children, foot→feet) |
| `m-l1-word-order` | Applies Urdu word order to English | English is subject–verb–object |
| `m-capitalization-random` | Random capitals | capital at sentence start + names only |

## 5. Assessment · revision · homework · project

- **Assessment:** auto-graded reading/grammar/vocabulary MCQs; **mentor-mediated** writing, speaking,
  inference. Summative mentor-mediated.
- **Revision:** per-unit + spaced re-checks; distinct items.
- **Homework:** one short daily English task (read a line, say a sentence).
- **Project (per term):** "My picture dictionary" — the learner makes a small illustrated word set on a
  safe theme (animals, food) with a simple English sentence each. Integrates RD/VO/WR/GR; mentor-
  assessed; resource-light.

---

## 6. Parent + teacher notes

- **Parents (2-minute helps):** no English required — encourage daily reading aloud; ask the child to
  name things in English; praise effort.
- **Teachers:** protect reading-comprehension validity (child reads, not audio); scaffold in Urdu but
  keep the English target; own mentor-mediated writing/speaking; log sticking points.
