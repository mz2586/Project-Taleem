Status: **Phase 7 · Grade 4 core subject · spine complete.** Authored to the
[Curriculum Framework](../../CURRICULUM_FRAMEWORK.md); `authored-original`, `[RE-EXPRESSED]`,
child-safe, Urdu-first. Objective prefix `URD-G4-*`.

> **Construct-validity note (important):** Urdu is the medium of instruction *and* a subject here.
> Where **reading is the construct** (reading-comprehension items), the passage carries **no audio

## 3. Exemplar lesson — ★ URD-G4-RD-03 (Reading comprehension)

- **Learning outcome `[RE-EXPRESSED]`:** the learner can answer literal questions about a short read
  Urdu passage.
- **Difficulty:** CORE · **Duration:** 20 min · **Prerequisites:** URD-G4-RD-01.
- **Vocabulary:** عبارت (passage), سوال (question), جواب (answer), خیال (idea).
- **Construct rule:** the **passage is read by the learner, not narrated** (no audio scaffolding on the
  passage). Instructions + feedback remain audio-first.
- **Worked example (script):** "پہلے پوری عبارت پڑھیں۔ پھر سوال پڑھیں۔ جواب عبارت میں سے تلاش کریں۔"
  (Read the whole passage first; then the question; find the answer in the text.)
- **Practice (≥ 5 items):** a short original passage (authored-original) + literal MCQs ("متن کے
  مطابق…"), one "which sentence tells us…" selector. Auto-graded.
- **Assessment:** 6-item pool; ≥ 4/5 distinct. **Revision triggers:** wrong on main-idea (RD-04);
  `m-guessing-not-reading`.
- **Misconception:** `m-guessing-not-reading` — answering from prior knowledge, not the text →
  correction: "جواب متن میں سے دیں، اپنی یاد سے نہیں" (answer from the text, not memory).
- **Hint ladder:** H1 "کیا لفظ عبارت میں ہے؟"; H2 "اُس جملے کو دوبارہ پڑھیں"; H3 re-teach with a
  parallel passage.
- **Homework:** read a short passage at home and tell a family member one fact from it.
- **Parent note:** ask your child to read a few lines aloud and say what happened.
- **Teacher note:** for comprehension items, confirm the child actually reads — don't let audio (or a
  helper) do the reading.

## 4. Misconception library (Urdu)

| Ref | Misconception | Correction (Urdu-first) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `m-guessing-not-reading` | Answers comprehension from memory, not the text | جواب متن میں سے دیں |
| `m-gender-default-male` | Treats all nouns as مذکر | ہر اسم کی جنس الگ ہے — یاد کریں یا نشانی دیکھیں |
| `m-tense-marker-ignored` | Ignores tense markers (گا/گی/تھا) | فعل کے آخر کی نشانی زمانہ بتاتی ہے |
| `m-plural-rule-overgeneralized` | Applies one plural rule to all nouns | جمع کے کئی طریقے ہیں (اں/یں/ات) |
| `m-letter-form-position` | Wrong letter form by position (initial/medial/final) | حرف کی شکل اُس کی جگہ سے بدلتی ہے |

## 5. Assessment · revision · homework · project

- **Assessment:** auto-graded reading/grammar/vocabulary MCQs; **mentor-mediated** writing, speaking,
  and inference (constructed). Summative mentor-mediated.
- **Revision:** per-unit revision + spaced re-checks of grammar/vocabulary with distinct items.
- **Homework:** one short daily reading/writing task tied to the lesson.
- **Project (per term):** "میری چھوٹی کتاب" — the learner writes + illustrates a 4–6 sentence booklet on
  a familiar, safe topic (family meal, my school day). Integrates RD/WR/GR/VO; mentor-assessed;
  resource-light; no personal data disclosed.

---

## 6. Parent + teacher notes

- **Parents (2-minute helps):** listen to your child read aloud; ask "what happened?"; name objects in
  Urdu; encourage neat letters.
- **Teachers:** protect reading-comprehension validity (child reads, not audio); reinforce the named
  misconceptions in person; own the mentor-mediated writing/speaking assessment; log sticking points.
