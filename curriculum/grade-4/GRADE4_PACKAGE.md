## 3. Academic-year plan (sequencing)

Grade 4 runs as **three terms**; each term ends with a per-subject revision checkpoint and a
mentor-mediated term review. Suggested cross-subject sequencing (short daily sessions):

| Term | Mathematics | Urdu / English | Science | Social Studies | Islamiat/Ethics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **T1** | Numbers, Add/Subtract | Reading + phonics, basic writing | Living things, Materials | My country + community | Foundations / core values |
| **T2** | Multiply, Divide, Fractions | Comprehension, grammar | Energy & forces | Geography + maps | Worship/akhlaq / honesty & kindness |
| **T3** | Measurement, Geometry, Data | Composition, speaking | Earth & space, Inquiry | Civics + basic economics | Seerah-stories / responsibility & respect |

Cross-subject coherence is deliberate: Math measurement ↔ Science inquiry; literacy (Urdu/English) ↔
every subject's reading load; Social Studies "my country" ↔ Islamiat/Ethics values. The full DAG is
declared per subject.

## 4. Assessment, revision, homework, projects (grade-wide)

- **Assessment:** continuous formative (every practice item is evidence); auto-graded Knowledge/
  Application; **mentor-mediated** constructed + summative. No answer keys ship to the device.
- **Revision:** per-unit revision lessons + spaced re-checks across the year with distinct items.
- **Homework:** one short, everyday, resource-light task per lesson.
- **Projects:** one small mentor-guided applied project per subject per term, integrating several
  objectives (and, where natural, more than one subject) — resource-light, safe, mentor-assessed.
