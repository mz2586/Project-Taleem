## 3. Exemplar lesson — ★ SOC-G4-ECO-01 (Needs vs wants)

- **Learning outcome `[RE-EXPRESSED]`:** the learner can tell a **need** (required to live/learn) from
  a **want** (nice to have).
- **Difficulty:** INTRO · **Duration:** 16 min · **Prerequisites:** none.
- **Vocabulary:** ضرورت (need), خواہش (want).
- **Worked example (script):** "پانی، کھانا، تعلیم — ضرورتیں ہیں۔ کھلونا، مٹھائی — خواہشیں ہیں۔"
  (Water, food, education are needs; a toy, sweets are wants.)
- **Practice (≥ 5 items):** sort familiar items into need/want; one "which is a NEED?" selector.
  Auto-graded. (Items are neutral + universal; no family-income references.)
- **Assessment:** 6-item pool. **Revision triggers:** `m-want-labelled-need`.
- **Misconception:** `m-want-labelled-need` — labels a strong want as a need → correction: "ضرورت وہ ہے
  جس کے بغیر گزارہ مشکل ہو" (a need is something you can't manage without).
- **Hint ladder:** H1 "کیا اِس کے بغیر گزارہ ہو سکتا ہے؟"; H2 "زندہ رہنے/سیکھنے کے لیے لازمی؟"; H3
  re-teach water vs toy.
- **Homework:** name one need and one want at home to a family member.
- **Parent note:** talk about needs vs wants while planning a simple shopping list.
- **Teacher note:** keep examples neutral + inclusive; avoid anything that could shame a child's
  circumstances.

## 4. Misconception library (Social Studies)

| Ref | Misconception | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `m-want-labelled-need` | A strong want is called a need | a need is essential to live/learn |
| `m-map-not-oriented` | Ignores map orientation/key | read the key + directions first |
| `m-rights-without-responsibility` | Rights without matching responsibility | every right has a responsibility |
| `m-helper-role-confusion` | Confuses community helpers' roles | match the helper to their job |

## 5. Assessment · revision · homework · project

- **Assessment:** auto-graded map/sort/match/MCQ; **mentor-mediated** any short opinion/discussion
  constructed items. Summative mentor-mediated.
- **Revision:** per-unit + spaced re-checks; distinct items.
- **Homework:** one short, neutral everyday task per lesson.
- **Project (per term):** "My community map" — the learner draws a simple map of a familiar, safe route
  (home to school) with directions + a key, and names two community helpers. Integrates GEO + CIV;
  mentor-assessed; **no home address or personal-location data is collected or shared** (safety).
