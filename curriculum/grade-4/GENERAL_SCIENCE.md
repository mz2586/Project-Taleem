## 3. Exemplar lesson — ★ SCI-G4-LIV-01 (Living vs non-living)

- **Learning outcome `[RE-EXPRESSED]`:** the learner can tell living from non-living things by their
  characteristics (grow, need food/water, move, respond).
- **Difficulty:** INTRO · **Duration:** 18 min · **Prerequisites:** none.
- **Vocabulary:** جاندار (living), بے جان (non-living), بڑھنا (grow), ضرورت (need).
- **Worked example (script):** "جاندار بڑھتے ہیں، کھاتے پیتے ہیں، حرکت کرتے ہیں۔ پودا جاندار ہے؛ پتھر بے
  جان ہے۔" (Living things grow, eat/drink, move. A plant is living; a stone is non-living.)
- **Practice (≥ 5 items):** "living or non-living?" for familiar items (plant, dog, stone, water,
  chair); one "which is NOT living?" selector. Auto-graded.
- **Assessment:** 6-item pool. **Revision triggers:** `m-moving-means-living`.
- **Misconception:** `m-moving-means-living` — thinks anything that moves is living (car, river) →
  correction: "حرکت کافی نہیں؛ جاندار بڑھتے اور کھاتے بھی ہیں" (movement alone isn't enough — living
  things also grow and eat).
- **Hint ladder:** H1 "کیا یہ بڑھتا ہے؟"; H2 "کیا یہ کھاتا/پیتا ہے؟"; H3 re-teach plant vs stone.
- **Homework:** at home, find two living and two non-living things and tell a family member.
- **Parent note:** point out plants/animals vs objects around the house.
- **Teacher note:** the "moving = living" misconception (cars, rivers) is the key one — address it.

## 4. Misconception library (Science)

| Ref | Misconception | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `m-moving-means-living` | Anything that moves is living | living things also grow + need food |
| `m-plants-not-living` | Plants aren't living (don't move fast) | plants grow, need water/light → living |
| `m-sun-moves-round-earth` | Sun moves around Earth causing day/night | Earth rotates; the Sun appears to move |
| `m-gas-is-nothing` | Gas/air is "nothing" | air is matter — it takes space + has weight |
| `m-heavier-always-sinks` | Heavier things always sink | it depends on the material/shape, not just weight |

## 5. Assessment · revision · homework · project

- **Assessment:** auto-graded classification/label/sequence MCQs; **mentor-mediated** inquiry
  (observation, prediction) constructed items. Summative mentor-mediated.
- **Revision:** per-unit + spaced re-checks; distinct items.
- **Homework:** one short, safe observation task per lesson.
- **Project (per term):** "My nature diary" — the learner observes + records (draw/write) a safe
  everyday phenomenon (a plant growing, shadows through the day, weather for a week). Integrates INQ +
  the term's content; mentor-assessed; **home-safe, no risky materials, no personal data**.

## 6. Parent + teacher notes

- **Parents (2-minute helps):** name living/non-living things; watch a shadow move; talk about weather;
  encourage safe observation — no experiments needing supervision-only materials.
- **Teachers:** keep all activities safe + resource-light; reinforce the named misconceptions;
  own the mentor-mediated inquiry assessment; log sticking points.
