## 3. Exemplar lesson — ★ ETH-G4-HON-01 (Why honesty matters) — Ethics track

- **Learning outcome `[RE-EXPRESSED]`:** the learner can explain why honesty matters and describe being
  truthful in everyday situations.
- **Difficulty:** INTRO · **Duration:** 16 min · **Prerequisites:** none.
- **Vocabulary:** سچائی (honesty/truth), بھروسا (trust).
- **Worked example (script):** "سچ بولنے سے لوگ ہم پر بھروسا کرتے ہیں۔ غلطی ہو جائے تو سچ بتانا بہادری
  ہے۔" (Telling the truth earns trust; owning a mistake honestly is brave.)
- **Practice (≥ 5 items):** "which choice is honest?" in gentle, familiar situations (returning
  something found, admitting a small mistake); one "what would an honest friend do?" selector.
  Auto-graded. (Situations are gentle — never shaming, never accusatory.)
- **Assessment:** 6-item pool. **Revision triggers:** `m-honesty-only-when-easy`.
- **Misconception:** `m-honesty-only-when-easy` — honesty only when convenient → correction: "سچ تب بھی
  بولتے ہیں جب مشکل ہو" (we're honest even when it's hard).
- **Hint ladder:** H1 "کیا اِس سے بھروسا بنتا ہے؟"; H2 "سچ کون سا ہے؟"; H3 re-teach with a gentle
  example.
- **Homework:** tell a family member about one honest thing you did today.
- **Parent note:** praise honesty warmly — especially when your child admits a mistake.
- **Teacher note:** keep every situation gentle + non-shaming; celebrate honesty, never interrogate.

*(The Islamiat-track exemplar ★ ISL-G4-AKH-01 mirrors this at the character level and is authored +
reviewed by a qualified religious subject-expert before publish.)*

## 4. Misconception library (values)

| Ref | Misconception | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `m-honesty-only-when-easy` | Be honest only when convenient | be honest even when it's hard |
| `m-kindness-to-friends-only` | Kindness only to friends | kindness to everyone, including animals |
| `m-respect-equals-fear` | Respect means being afraid | respect is care + good manners, not fear |
| `m-responsibility-someone-else` | "Someone else will do it" | each of us has a share of responsibility |

## 5. Assessment · revision · homework · project

- **Assessment:** auto-graded value-recognition MCQs; **mentor-mediated** oral retelling + reflection
  (constructed). Summative mentor-mediated. Situations are always gentle + non-shaming.
- **Revision:** per-unit + spaced re-checks; distinct items.
- **Homework:** one short, kind, everyday action tied to the value.
- **Project (per term):** "My kindness week" — the learner does + records small, safe good actions
  (help at home, be kind to a sibling/animal, tidy a shared space). Integrates the term's values;
  mentor-assessed; **no personal-data collection; nothing that pressures or shames.**
