## What the ingest carries

- **From the spine (every draft):** objective code (`learning_outcomes`, `provenance.aligned_slo_codes`, `ai_teaching_object.learning_goals`), the `[RE-EXPRESSED]` outcome (English `description`), difficulty, duration, resolved prerequisites, and placement plus competency and assessment type as `metadata.tags`.
- **Titles:** mathematics titles are the bilingual titles in `LESSON_CATALOG.md`. The other subjects' documents state no lesson title, so the English title is the outcome and the Urdu title is left empty.
- **Imported verbatim (provisional):** from each ★ exemplar section and each `GRADE4_MATH_CURRICULUM.md` §7 lesson: vocabulary pairs, worked-example *scripts*, the quoted H1/H2 hints, the referenced misconception-library rows, homework, and parent and teacher notes (`parent_notes`, `mentor_notes`). For `MATH-G4-FR-01`, the authored fixture `vertical_slice/fractions_lesson.py` (scripts, explanation, practice items with answer keys, hints, misconceptions, homework, notes, AI-teaching object).
- **Never invented:** text is split into Urdu and English only where the document writes both. A side it does not write stays empty and shows up below as a `title`/notes blocker. Practice and assessment lines in the documents are item *specifications* with no item wording, so no items are created from them.
- **Not imported from the fixture:** audio pointers, the `m-fraction-circle` media reference, the offline-package id, and its accessibility note (which states audio and an image are provided). None of those assets exist in the repository.
- **Marking:** every imported lesson carries `provisional`, `content-status:provisional-pending-review`, `imported-from:<doc §section>` and a `provenance-note:` tag, plus an `import-hash:<field>=…` fingerprint per field. A field an author has edited since is never overwritten (it is reported instead).
- **AI drafts:** `curriculum/grade-4/ai-drafts/<code>.json` (format and rules in `tools/curriculum_ai_drafts.py`). A draft fills only fields the human sources leave empty, plus the missing language of a human field written in one language. Each lesson it touches is tagged `ai-drafted`, `content-status:ai-draft-pending-review` and `ai-drafted-field:<name>` per field. Drafts carry no audio, media or offline-package pointer.
- **Hierarchy:** grade `G4` → subjects → chapters (units) → topics (unit × domain) is stored under the `PK-NCP` pack through `CurriculumStudioService.upsert_hierarchy` (6 subjects, 37 chapters, 39 topics). Every draft links to its chapter and topic by `metadata.chapter_key` / `metadata.topic_key`; the `unit:`/`topic:` tags remain.
